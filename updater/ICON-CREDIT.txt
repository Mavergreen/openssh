Updater icon: the GENERIC macOS application icon, on purpose.

OpenSSHUpdater ships no bundled icon (empty CFBundleIconFile), so macOS renders it with the
standard generic application icon. This is the explicit opt-in: CMakeLists.txt passes
ALLOW_GENERIC to mavericks_add_updater_app (equivalently -DMAVERICKS_ALLOW_GENERIC_ICON=ON).

Why generic rather than bespoke artwork: OpenSSH has no official logo, and the marks one might
reach for are encumbered -- the OpenBSD "Puffy" mascot is license-restricted (like Docker's whale
that mavericks-container-tools steered around), and other SSH clients' icons (PuTTY, etc.) are
their own copyright. Rather than commission original art for a CLI updater, we ship the generic
system icon and embed no third-party artwork at all -- no Apple, OpenBSD, or vendor mark, no
attribution requirement.

To give it a real icon later: drop a proper .icns in this dir and pass ICON <path> instead of
ALLOW_GENERIC in CMakeLists.txt.
